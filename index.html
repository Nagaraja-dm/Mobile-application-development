Frequently Asked Questions

How much does mobile app development cost?

Cost depends on features, platforms, integrations and design effort. After a short discovery call we share a clear scope and estimate, so you know what you are paying for before work begins.

How long does it take to build an app?

A focused first version usually takes a few months. Timelines depend on scope, integrations and approvals. We agree on milestones up front and share progress at each one.

Should I build for iOS, Android or both?

It depends on where your users are. We look at your audience, budget and goals, then recommend one platform first or both together.

Do you build cross-platform apps?

Yes. Cross-platform development uses a shared foundation for iOS and Android. It suits many business apps when speed and consistent upkeep matter. We choose native or cross-platform based on your product and users.

Can the app integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. We connect apps to CRM, ERP, identity systems, analytics, payment gateways and other platforms through secure APIs, so your data stays in sync.

How do you keep the app secure?

We plan security from the start: secure sign-in and role-based permissions, encrypted communication, protected APIs and controlled distribution for private apps.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Yes. We support your app after launch with analytics, fixes, updates and continuous improvement, so it keeps working as devices and operating systems change.

Who owns the app and source code?

Ownership and intellectual property terms are agreed in your project contract before work begins. Ask us about this during your free consultation.
